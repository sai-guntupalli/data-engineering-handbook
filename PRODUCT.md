# Product

**Name:** Data Engineering Handbook

**What it is:** An open handbook of Jupyter notebooks for data engineering. Each topic is a notebook with explanations,
runnable examples and self-checks. Every notebook opens in Google Colab with no local setup.

**Who it is for:** Engineers preparing for data-engineering interviews, and engineers learning a tool such as Spark,
SQL, Kafka or Airflow for the first time. They arrive from search or a link, scan for a topic, then run a notebook.

**Success:** A visitor finds the notebook they need within seconds and opens it in Colab. Browsing and searching must be
fast and calm, because the visitor is studying, often on a laptop, sometimes on a phone.

**Tone:** Plain, precise and unhurried, like a good textbook. No marketing language.

**Constraints:** Static site on GitHub Pages. Light and dark themes. Keyboard and screen-reader access. Content comes
from the notebooks and must not change in the redesign.
